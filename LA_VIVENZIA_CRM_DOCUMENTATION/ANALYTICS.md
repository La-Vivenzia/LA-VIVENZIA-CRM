# Analytics

## Executive metrics
Total leads, new leads, qualified leads, onboardings, approved partners, activated partners, conversion, response time, follow-up compliance, onboarding completion and team productivity.

## Dimensions
Source, category, geography, owner, team, time period, status, score and onboarding state.

## Funnel
Discovery → Lead → Qualification → Engagement → Conversion → Onboarding → Activation → Growth, with drill-down.

## Team
Leads handled, response time, follow-up compliance, qualification, conversion, onboarding completion, stuck leads and SLA breaches.

## Management intelligence
Best sources, high-converting categories, geographic opportunities, onboarding bottlenecks, slow-response areas, repeated problems and workload imbalance.

## Phase 3
Conversion forecasting, onboarding volume, inactivity risk, capacity, demand and opportunity forecasting.

## Daily brief
New leads, high-potential leads, onboardings, activated partners, stuck leads, overdue work, top performer and recommended management action.

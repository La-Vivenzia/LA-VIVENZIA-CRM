# Communications

## Channels
Email, phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn, video call, meeting and internal notes.

## Email
Use official La Vivenzia accounts where configured. Track Sent → Received → Replied → Follow-up and associate threads with the relationship.

## Email AI
Summarise, detect intent/objections, draft response, suggest follow-up, create task and attach thread to lead.

## Communication record
Channel, timestamp, actor, duration, purpose, summary, sentiment, outcome, objections, commitments, next action and next follow-up.

## Brand voice
Human, premium, clear, specific, warm without being generic and commercially responsible.

## Approval
Human approval for commercial terms, approval/rejection decisions, sensitive escalations, legal/compliance messages and high-risk external communication.

# AI Intelligence

AI is an internal operational capability, not the product differentiator.

## Responsibilities
Discover, classify, qualify, assist, remember, remind, verify, analyse, predict and recommend.

## Lead intelligence
Identify who, offering, location, category, relevance, why onboard, potential offer, missing information and next action.

## Opportunity score
0–100 based on strategic relevance, uniqueness, location, quality, demand, digital presence, reviews/reputation, completeness, customer appeal and partnership potential. Always explain the score.

## Relationship memory
Summarise relationship context, interests, objections, communication preference, availability, last meaningful event and next step.

## Conversation assistant
Turn rough notes into sentiment, objections, commercial concern, requested information, recommended response, next action and follow-up.

## Communication assistant
Email, WhatsApp, Instagram, follow-up, meeting invite, explanation and objection responses. Controls: Professional, Friendly, Shorter, Persuasive, Human, Translate, Explain Simply, Follow-up.

## Next Best Action
Call, WhatsApp, email, schedule, request document, research, escalate or complete.

## Data quality
Detect missing information, duplicate candidates, wrong category, suspicious data, invalid contact information, inconsistent names and missing mandatory fields. AI suggests; humans confirm.

## Phase 3 forecasting
Conversion, onboarding volume, inactivity, capacity, workload and category/geography opportunity.

## Guardrails
No fabricated facts, autonomous partner approval, deletion, unapproved ownership change, commercial commitments or sensitive communication without required human approval.

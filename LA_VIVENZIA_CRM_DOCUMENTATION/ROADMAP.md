# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Clean fork, Git, baseline verification, licensing review, CI, UI tokens, LV modules and confirmed data model.

## Phase 1 — Operational CRM MVP
Login, roles, lead database, dynamic categories, capture, universal profile, timeline, assignment, tasks, follow-ups, interaction history, dashboards, partner onboarding, email, search, admin controls, audit, restricted deletion, notifications and SLA.

## Phase 2 — Intelligence
Classification, scoring, duplicate similarity, relationship summaries, email/WhatsApp drafting, next-best-action, problem classification, onboarding assistance, OCR, daily brief and data quality intelligence.

## Phase 3 — Intelligent Operating System
Voice capture, advanced analytics, predictive conversion, partner health, workflow builder extensions, forecasting, relationship graph, advanced integrations and low-risk automated intelligence.

## Priority
Do not optimise autonomy before Phase 1 reliability is strong. Every lead must be captured, owned, followed up, traceable and auditable.
